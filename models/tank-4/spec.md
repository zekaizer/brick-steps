# 탱크 4호

## Purpose

Tank 1 with rhombus tracks, after the First World War's Mark IV: each track climbs to a high front horn, so the tank can put its nose on a step, and a sprung road wheel lets the ground run give a little.

Published in the gallery on 2026-11-28, before a real build, at the maintainer's request. The open questions below are still open.

## Mechanism

- The drive is tank 1's: two large motors side by side turn the sprockets at (y 0, z 0). Two upright 11 x 7 frames carry the rear sprockets, now in the frames' holes at (y -40, z 280), and the hub on two 9L beams. Tank 1's hand-turned turret is left out: the horn beams pass where it turned.
- Horn idlers: a 13L beam inside each frame (y -80, pinned to the frame at z 80, 120 and 160) reaches forward to z -60 and carries an 8L axle with a 36-tooth gear as the idler at (y -80, z -60). An axle joiner and a bush fill the axle between the beam and the gear, so the idler cannot slide inwards.
- Suspension: a 5L arm swings on a plain pin in each frame's hole at (y 0, z 80) and carries a 36-tooth gear as a road wheel at (y 0, z 160). A small rubber band (`85543`) between a pin on the arm (z 140) and a pin in the frame (y 40, z 160) pulls the arm down, so the road wheel presses the ground run and takes up the track's slack. A bush between the arm and the road wheel keeps the wheel in line. Both band pins go in from outside before the road wheel covers them, and the band goes on before the road wheel.
- The track is 35 links per side around the four wheels, generated by `scripts/gen_tank_rhombus_track.py`. The loop is 1033.6 LDU, so 35 links of 30 leave about 16 LDU of slack for the road wheel to take up.
- Steering is differential, as in tank 1. The missions are tank 1's (`missions.yaml`).

## Ports

Declared in `model.mpd` as `0 !LC PORT` lines; `lc validate` checks them.

## Open questions

None of this has been built. To confirm on the real parts:

- **Link count.** 35 links per side is 70 in all, 12 more than tank 1. The tank-tracks set's count is unknown; it may not have that many.
- **Gears as idlers.** The 36-tooth gears stand in for sprockets at the horn and the road wheel. Their teeth do not match the link pitch, so they work as rollers; check that the links ride on them without jumping off.
- **Rubber band.** The band is drawn as a loose ring; how hard it pulls, and whether 85543 (15 mm) or a larger belt suits it, only a build shows. The band stays inside the track loop near the ground run.
- **Play on the 36-tooth gears.** As on tank 1's sprockets, each idler and road wheel has 8 LDU between its hub and the bush on either side: the track runs at x ±148, off the frames' 20 LDU grid. Check that the track keeps them in line.
- **Arm stops.** Nothing stops the arm at the bottom of its swing except the track; with too little slack the road wheel stays up and the suspension does nothing.
- **Horn beams.** Each 13L beam hangs 130 LDU in front of the frame on three pins; the horn takes the push when the tank climbs.
- Everything listed for tank 1 (axle depth, frame rigidity, drive direction) still applies.
- `scripts/ldraw_measure.py overlap tank-4` lists the gears against the track links, as for sprockets, and the drive sprocket against the horn gear, whose boxes meet; their circles are 2 LDU apart.
