# 탱크 4호

## Purpose

Tank 1 with trapezoid tracks, after the First World War's Mark IV: each track climbs to a high front horn, so the tank can put its nose on a step. To get over the step the hull has to clear it too, so the hull sits high between the tracks.

Published in the gallery on 2026-11-28, before a real build, at the maintainer's request. The open questions below are still open.

## Mechanism

- Ground clearance (ground at y 62): between the frames nothing comes below y -30, 94 LDU (about 38 mm) of clearance. The frames and the outer beams beside the tracks stay above y 10, 52 LDU. Before revision 3 the motors and the beam between them were 12 to 14 LDU above the ground.
- Drive: two large motors side by side sit high at the rear with their outputs on the rear axles at (y -80, z 280), as tank 1's motors sit on its front axles, so the drive turns the same way as tank 1's. Two beams between the motors (y -120 and -40) and 3L pins hold them together.
- Frames: two upright 11 x 7 frames at y -130..10, z 30..250, in front of the motors' output bosses. Each is pinned to its motor on the frame's top row (z 160, 200). The hub sits on two 9L beams across the frames' top edges (z 60, 180).
- Horn idlers: a 9L beam inside each frame on its top row (y -120, pinned at z 40 and 80) reaches forward to z -60 and carries an 8L axle with a 36-tooth gear as the idler. An axle joiner and a bush fill the axle between the beam and the gear.
- Ground run: a 13L beam outside each frame, pinned to the frame's bottom row (y 0, z 40, 80, 200), carries the front idler (a sprocket at z 0) and a 36-tooth gear as the road wheel at z 220. The ground run reaches from z 0 to z 220, so the centre of gravity (z 159) is 67 LDU inside the rear edge of the contacts.
- The track is 37 links per side around the four wheels (horn idler, rear sprocket, road wheel, front idler), generated by `scripts/gen_tank_rhombus_track.py`. The loop is 1101.2 LDU, so 37 links of 30 leave about 9 LDU of slack. No wheel is sprung: the sprung road wheel of revisions 1 and 2 needed a band anchored below the arm, and the raised frames leave nothing there.
- Steering is differential, as in tank 1. The missions are tank 1's (`missions.yaml`).

## Ports

Declared in `model.mpd` as `0 !LC PORT` lines; `lc validate` checks them.

## Open questions

None of this has been built. To confirm on the real parts:

- **Link count.** 37 links per side is 74 in all, 16 more than tank 1. The tank-tracks set's count is unknown; it may not have that many.
- **Slack.** About 9 LDU (3.5 mm) of slack with no sprung wheel. Check that the track does not jump the sprocket teeth; if it does, the road wheel can move to the beam hole at z 240 for a tighter loop once the rear sprocket clears it (at z 240 their circles meet).
- **Gears as idlers.** The 36-tooth gears stand in for sprockets at the horn and the road wheel. Their teeth do not match the link pitch, so they work as rollers; check that the links ride on them without jumping off.
- **Play on the 36-tooth gears.** As on tank 1's sprockets, each idler and road wheel has 8 LDU between its hub and the bush on either side: the track runs at x ±148, off the frames' 20 LDU grid. Check that the track keeps them in line.
- **Rear axles.** Each rear sprocket hangs on its motor's output alone, as tank 1's front sprockets do, and the motors carry the frames through two pins each. Check that the rear end does not sag or twist under load.
- **Road wheel axle.** It turns in the outer beam's hole only, with its end against the frame's side (`ldraw_measure.py axles` reports it `touches-only`).
- **Horn beams.** Each 9L beam reaches 100 LDU in front of the frame on two pins; the horn takes the push when the tank climbs.
- Everything listed for tank 1 (axle depth, frame rigidity, drive direction) still applies.
- `scripts/ldraw_measure.py overlap tank-4` lists the 36-tooth gears against the track links, as for sprockets, and the rear sprocket against the road wheel, whose boxes meet; their circles are 2 LDU apart.
