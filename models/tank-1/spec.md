# 탱크 1호

## Purpose

A first, simple tracked robot: two motors, two tracks, a hub and a small turret turned by hand.

## Mechanism

- Two large motors lie side by side with their outputs facing outward. Two 7L beams between them, with 3L pins, tie them together.
- Each motor turns a sprocket directly through a 7L axle. An upright 11 x 7 frame is pinned to the side of each motor and carries the idler sprocket 280 LDU behind the drive axle.
- Two 9L beams across the frames carry the hub.
- The track is 29 links per side, generated by `scripts/gen_tank_track.py`.
- The turret is a cross block on a single plain pin, with two 5L axles as barrels. It turns freely by hand. The head is its own sub-assembly (`turret-head.ldr`, `0 !LC TURN`), and the box check finds nothing in its way over a full turn. The tracks are the floor contact (`0 !LC GROUND`); the sprockets clear the floor by 13 LDU.
- Steering is differential. The SPIKE App's movement blocks drive motors A (left) and B (right) as a pair; the missions are in `missions.yaml`.

## Ports

Declared in `model.mpd` as `0 !LC PORT` lines; `lc validate` checks them.

## Open questions

None of this has been built. To confirm on the real parts:

- **Track length.** The loop is 864.7 LDU and 29 links of 30 LDU are 870, so the track should be slightly loose. The 30 LDU pitch and the third-party links are unverified; the count may be 28 or 30.
- **Drive axle depth.** The 7L axle is drawn 40 LDU deep in the motor. If the motor accepts less, the sprocket sits further out.
- **Frame rigidity.** Each frame hangs on three pins at its front end, and the idler is 200 LDU behind them. It may need a brace to the hub beams.
- **Motor direction.** The left motor is mirrored, which is what the app's movement blocks expect for A+B. If the tank drives backwards on "forward", swap the ports in `set_movement_motors`.
- **Turning.** Mission 3 assumes one rotation turns the tank roughly a quarter; the child finds the real number. Write the measured value back into the mission once a build shows it.
- **Cables.** The motors are drawn without cables. Both cables have to reach ports A and B at the hub.
- **Turret.** One plain pin holds it; it may be too loose.
- `scripts/ldraw_measure.py overlap tank-1` reports each motor against its frame. That is the box of the output boss; the motor body ends at the frame's inner face.
